# jps Telephone 3.7 (166) — history, recovery and recording management

Includes the completed source improvements since 3.6.1: expanded history find/type/account filters; Contacts permission and lookup recovery; truthful pending/slow audio feedback; recording start/fallback and actual-folder feedback; event-driven call controls; selectable asynchronous recording recovery; and exact call/segment recording attachments with Play in Default App and Reveal in Finder.

Recording recovery preserves original audio after failure. A durable catalogue and export journal support metadata-only link repair, with ownership, no-overwrite and quit-drain checks. History deletion preserves recordings. Old calls remain valid and unlinked; no filename guesses are made. The current SIP activity policy was measured and retained, with no battery claim.

The compact dialler/Settings/call layouts, provider routing and existing user data remain unchanged. This is an Apple-silicon macOS15.6+ ad-hoc signed, unnotarised release. Actual provider/hardware, permission/save-panel grants, playback handoff and proficient VoiceOver acceptance remain pending in #62. A conservative parent-directory-sync failure retains encoded output plus raw tracks and reports recovery pending, including for some file-only save grants.
