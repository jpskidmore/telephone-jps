## Saved data and durable recovery

Unreadable or malformed history stays intact until a successful reload can merge
queued calls. Clear Call History commits deletion before showing an empty list;
failed clearing remains retryable. Removed accounts reject delayed writes, and
old Contacts/history results cannot undo a newer update. Quit drains admitted
history and recording work before its final reply.

A recording export failure retains protected raw audio and a durable retry job.
Recover Recordings offers Retry, Save Elsewhere, Show Recovery Files or Later.
A fresh destination is required when original metadata is unusable or would be
removed with the raw job; successful conversion alone removes the originals.
See [recording/history recovery](../RECORDING_RECOVERY.md).

## SIP, audio and asynchronous ownership

Audited native SDK operations run on the serial backend worker, with main-thread
admission and endpoint/call/selection generation checks. Pending work cannot
silently reopen a stopped endpoint, removed account or replaced call. Startup,
Offline and Network-save intent is applied consistently without restart loops.
Transfer results reach the right source window, failed local hold/hangup retains
controls, ringback resets correctly and codec changes wait for media readiness.

Physical CoreAudio UIDs identify audio devices even when display names match.
Hotplug mapping failures after Null sound remain visibly failed until an explicit
retry succeeds. Repeated implicit events cannot repeatedly open a failed driver.
The UI stays responsive while controlled worker operations are held; a native
operation may still stall its worker. See [audio recovery](../AUDIO_RECOVERY.md)
and [driver provenance](../DEPENDENCY_BUILDS.md).

## Compact interface, accounts and resources

Keyboard/dead-key/DTMF input, hold timers, transfer redial/observer ownership and
visible-window placement are corrected. Native accessibility purpose labels,
account validation and credential error handling retain the user's inputs instead
of silently committing a failed change. First-run readiness and later external
call requests follow accepted account creation. Expired subscribers and native
resource/background activity/PJLIB ownership are cleaned up; unused legacy
categories are removed.

The accepted layout remains compact: Icon rail Settings with Titled sections at
one Accounts-sized window, Dial bar with one recent call, Pocket call cards,
native close controls, UK English, Light/Dark, green Online and red Offline.
Calling, recording, accounts, history and settings functions are retained.
