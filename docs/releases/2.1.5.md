# jps Telephone 2.1.5 (build 157)

Compact desktop interface, 7 October 2026. Apple silicon, macOS 15.6+.

## Compact windows

The previous interface added a wide Settings sidebar and enlarged call windows.
This release follows the selected compact layouts while retaining the existing
calling, recording, account and settings behavior.

- **Settings: Icon rail.** General, Accounts, Sound and Network use a narrow,
  edge-to-edge icon rail with tooltips and accessible names. Each pane fits its
  contents; the wide sidebar and shared minimum height are removed. Fields keep
  their labels. Accounts retains its account list, editing restrictions, detail
  tabs and ordering. Network retains its save, cancel and discard flow.
- **Main window: Dial bar with one recent call.** Availability, number entry and
  calling controls stay at the top. One recent call remains visible underneath.
  Full history expands on request, preserving the existing history actions and
  separate account windows.
- **Calls: Pocket card.** A compact, original-width card shows caller identity,
  number, status, duration and recording state. A single icon row provides Mute,
  Hold, Transfer, Keypad and End. Incoming and ended calls retain Answer/Decline
  and Call Back. The keypad appears on request; physical-keyboard DTMF and the
  existing transfer sheets remain available.

Native window controls remain visible. Settings retains its opaque semantic
titlebar. Native regular Liquid Glass is reserved for navigation and controls;
the content uses standard system materials and semantic colors for light and
dark appearance. Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast remain supported.
On macOS 27, system material refinements apply through the native controls.

## UK English

The interface uses UK English only. German and Russian resources and their
language-specific defaults are removed. Dates use UK day/month order and
24-hour time, and durations use English units. Saved number-formatting choices,
international phone numbers, contact names and provider diagnostic text for
unknown SIP response codes remain available. Store prices retain their currency
formatting, and receipt and recording formats retain their protocol requirements.

## Verification and local testing

The release pipeline checks dependency integrity, audio and recording harnesses,
Foundation regressions, all four native test suites, the arm64 Release build,
ad-hoc signatures and the packaged app after extraction. The
bundle check also rejects non-English translations and verifies UK English
development languages across the app and its frameworks. The generated
BUILD-VERIFICATION.md identifies the exact source commit and completed checks.
The packaged application is inspected locally before publication.

This is an ad-hoc signed, unnotarized local test build. Live SIP, actual audio
devices, VoiceOver and full OS coverage remain manual acceptance tests.

Quit the previous edition before opening the new app. Its identity remains
uk.jpsit.telephone, retaining existing settings and credentials. Keep the
previous download until local call/audio testing passes.
